# Governance

- **Steward:** VDHome (BE 0757.674.324) holds the name and the release keys and appoints maintainers.
- **Maintainers** ([MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md)) decide what is merged and released. Until there is a second
  maintainer, the maintainer merges their own pull requests after CI; from the second maintainer on, every pull request
  needs an approval from someone other than its author.
- **Decisions** are recorded in `docs/decisions/`. Anyone may propose one in an issue; the maintainer answers in
  writing.
- **Appeal:** open an issue that names the decision and why it should change; the maintainer responds in writing within
  30 days.
- **A new maintainer** is proposed in an issue after sustained contributions and appointed by the steward.
